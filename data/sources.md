# Data Sources

| # | Dataset | Agency | Year | Link |
|---|---------|--------|------|------|
| 1 | PLACES: Census Tract Data (GIS Friendly Format) | CDC | 2024 release | [data.cdc.gov](https://data.cdc.gov/500-Cities-Places/PLACES-Census-Tract-Data-GIS-Friendly-Format-2024-/yjkw-uj5s) |
| 2 | Washington Census Tract Boundaries (TIGER/Line) | U.S. Census Bureau | 2023 | [census.gov](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/tiger-line-file.html) |

The CDC PLACES dataset provides model-based estimates of health outcomes, behaviors, and access to care at the census tract level across the United States. For this project, the dataset was filtered to Washington State only (~1,770 census tracts). All values are crude prevalence percentages (not age-adjusted).
